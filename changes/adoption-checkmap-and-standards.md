- `docs/adoption.md` - new: the **adoption checkmap** - a rigid, ordered set of gates every repo passes to reach the standard (greenfield by interview, brownfield by reading the code). Each gate produces a document and/or tasks; the path ends with a **counted backlog** ("N tasks to full alignment") and a green `self-verify`. Includes guidance on **which model to drive it with** (a top-tier reasoning model as orchestrator at high thinking, cheap bounded sub-agents fanned out for mechanical per-capability work) - what is rigid vs. the agent's call during adaptation.
- **Cross-discipline standards folded in** (same pattern as personas): **C4** as the `ARCHITECTURE.md` diagram convention; **WCAG 2.2 AA** as an accessibility baseline in the Quality catalog; **INVEST + Definition of Ready** in the backlog template; **Impact Mapping + Story Mapping** in `greenfield-start` (goal -> persona -> module); **OWASP ASVS + SLSA** referenced in the security baseline. Scrum/SAFe deliberately left out - the framework is spec-driven, not ceremony-based.
