- Conventions gain a **working-language policy**: because an AI reads whatever language you choose, the standard makes natural language a **per-artifact config**, not an assumption. Default is English (widest collaboration, best ecosystem fit), but any team may pick its own - a German team writing German docs and English commits is a first-class setup. Declared in `AGENTS.md`, catalogued as a project decision, and user-facing copy follows the persona's language, not the team's.
