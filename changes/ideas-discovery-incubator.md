headline: Ideas and discovery are first-class - explore before you decide
---
- The taxonomy now blesses **speculative ideas/discovery** as a first-class kind: an idea that may never ship can be explored end-to-end - including its provisional technical and business shape - in a discovery doc, without minting any ADR/BDR/spec. Records and specs are created only when an idea is **approved for realization**; `Proposed` is for a decision awaiting ratification, not for a maybe. The full division (folder, lifecycle, graduation, guard) is scoped as backlog `IDEA-1`.
