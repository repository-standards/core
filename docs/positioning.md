## Positioning statement

For **teams and solo builders who run their repositories with AI agents**, who need
product knowledge - docs, specs, decisions - to stay true instead of scattering across
tools, **repository-standards** is a **living, versioned, self-verifying engineering
standard**: point a repo at a version and it aligns, guides the build, and proves
compliance - `align -> verify -> drift as a number`. Unlike SDD workflows (Spec Kit,
OpenSpec, BMAD) or scaffolds (starters, golden paths), it is a **reference the repo
trues up to** - with a guided, re-entrant brownfield transition, so the messy repo you
already have is a first-class citizen.

## The three messaging pillars

1. **Executable, not aspirational.** A standard written to be *run* by an agent -
   hooks, gates, and a compliance number your CI asserts - not a wall document.
   (Proof: `self-verify`, the clarify gate, the coupling guard.)
2. **One repo, everything coherent.** Code, specs, decisions, product, personas -
   versioned together, updated in the same PR, readable by humans and loadable by
   agents. Proximity is the mechanism; coherence is the outcome. (Proof: living docs,
   same-PR coupling, the taxonomy.)
3. **It walks you there.** Greenfield scaffolds and goes; brownfield is guided in
   prioritized waves, re-entered until drift 0 - hand-holding is the product. (Proof:
   re-entrant align, the guided loop, statuses with the clarify gate.)
