# Personas stay a hard gate even solo

**Decided:** in the profiles split, the gate is core, the full roster is scale;
a capability spec that serves nobody fails the structure guard (R10, ADR-006).

**Doubt:** for a one-person tool the ceremony may still exceed the value - and
for repos with no product surface at all (an internal service, a library) a
persona gate may deserve a separate product profile rather than core.

**A better answer would:** a solo case study showing the one-persona gate caught
(or never caught) a real mistake.
